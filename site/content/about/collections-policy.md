## Mission {#mission}

The mission of the Ace Archive project is to document and preserve the histories
of queer communities whose histories are underdocumented, with a particular
focus on communities in the asexual and aromantic spectrum.

## Values {#values}

### Equitable {#equitable}

Equity means affirming identity, honoring self-determination, uplifting
marginalized voices, and challenging harmful power structures and social
hierarchies.

### Durable {#durable}

The project should be resilient to changes in funding, technology,
infrastructure, and maintainership. The collection should outlast the project.

### Accessible {#accessible}

The collection should be made available and accessible to as many people as
possible. Barriers to access are a social problem, and we lead with the belief
that they can be overcome.

### Transparent {#transparent}

Transparency in decision-making builds community trust. Transparency is
prerequisite to accountability.

### Collaborative {#collaborative}

We can only effect radical change by standing together. Building community is
not in service of the mission; it is the mission.
